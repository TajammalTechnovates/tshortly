## 📌 What is TShortly? — What does this project do?

TShortly turns long URLs into short, clean links instantly and for free — no registration needed for basic use. It offers secure storage, fast redirection, custom short links for your brand, click analytics and a simple developer API to automate shortening inside your own apps.

## 🎯 Who is it for?

Marketers, businesses, creators and developers who share links in campaigns, bios, SMS and social media.
